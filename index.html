FAQ - OpenPapers

OpenPapers is a Australia-wide, community-run, open-source past paper repository. Students upload trial, assessment, and exam papers from their schools, and the community verifies them via upvotes. All files are hosted on Google Drive CDN for fast downloads. No paywalls, no ads on downloads.
Downloading is 100% free and requires no account. You only need an account to upload papers or vote to help verify papers. We don't show ads on download pages and we never limit your downloads.
Every paper starts as Unverified. When it gets 15+ votes and 90%+ upvotes, it becomes Verified. If 70%+ downvotes, it's automatically binned. This community voting weeds out incorrect or spam files, even Drive files can be binned if community downvotes.
These sites are great for finding past papers, but for example Studocu, are filled with ads and have limits, such as requiring you to login to view files, which could be annoying for some, while the known NSW Trial past paper site, THSC online, is great but when traffic is high, will lead to loading the files super slow and annoying. OpenPapers runs on Google's CDN, ensuring blazing fast downloads. Moreover, other states does not have such a resource, and OpenPapers is built to breach the gap.
Uploaders must ensure they have permission to share. If you are a copyright holder, email 2uh114cu@duck.com with a DMCA request and we remove immediately. We log IPs at signup only to block spam.
