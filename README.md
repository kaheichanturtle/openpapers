# OpenPapers

Welcome to **OpenPapers**, a community-driven project built to make high school exam preparation easier, fairer, and completely free for students across Australia.

Preparing for Year 12 exams is stressful enough without having to hunt down past practice papers across dozens of sketchy websites, dead links, or paid paywalls. OpenPapers brings together a massive archive of trial exams, practice papers, and assessment tasks into one clean, fast, and easy-to-navigate platform.

## What's Inside?

We organize practice papers, trial exams, and solutions across major Australian senior secondary frameworks:

* **VIC** — VCE
* **QLD** — QCE
* **WA** — WACE
* **SA** — SACE
* **TAS** — TCE
* **ACT** — BSSS
* **NT** — NTCET
* **NSW** — HSC

---

## Key Features

* **Fast Browsing** — Instantly navigate through States, Subjects, and Tasks without annoying ads or endless pop-ups.
* **Direct Downloads** — Preview or download past trial papers and worked solutions in high-quality PDF format with one click.
* **Community-Driven Uploads** — Built by students, for students. Easily contribute papers from your school to help out future year groups.
* **Upvote & Moderation System** — Quality control powered by the community. Upvote complete papers with clear solutions and flag duplicates or corrupted files.

---

## How to Contribute

OpenPapers relies on students sharing resources to keep the library growing! If you have practice papers, trial exams, or sample solutions sitting on your laptop:

1. **Upload via the App** — Head to the upload page, pick the state, system, and subject, and drag in your PDF.
2. **Spread the Word** — Tell your classmates, study groups, and friends! The more papers shared, the better prepared everyone is.
